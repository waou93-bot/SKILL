# Image Direction DNA

## Core rule

Images are part of the art direction, not placeholders.

## Preferred qualities

- high-resolution native assets;
- strong framing;
- deliberate crop;
- visual consistency;
- cinematic or editorial treatment when appropriate;
- clear subject hierarchy;
- coherent lighting/temperature within one sequence;
- project-specific imagery instead of generic stock.

## When using generated images

Generated imagery must look intentionally art-directed and internally consistent.
Do not accept obvious model artifacts, arbitrary styles, mismatched faces, malformed products, fake UI or inconsistent lighting.

## Image use

Prefer:

- large decisive imagery;
- image sequences with narrative logic;
- reveal/mask behavior tied to scroll;
- product screenshots that show something real;
- fewer excellent images over many mediocre ones.

Avoid:

- filling every card with an unrelated image;
- low-resolution assets stretched to appear premium;
- gratuitous grain;
- decorative stock photography;
- image treatment that damages legibility;
- automatic AI upscale as a substitute for a good source asset.
